```{r}
library(tidyverse)

rates <- read_csv("unemployment.csv")
REGION <- "Jazan"
now <- rates |>
  filter(region == REGION, quarter == "2025Q4") |>
  pull(rate)
then <- rates |>
  filter(region == REGION, quarter == "2022Q1") |>
  pull(rate)
```

## Summary

::: {.column-margin}
Rates are a share of the labour force, in %.
:::

Unemployment **fell** in most regions between 2022 and 2025.
*Jazan* is the exception. This note shows the national
trend (@fig-trend) and the latest rates
(@tbl-latest).[^1]

In `r REGION` the rate was
`r scales::number(now, accuracy = 0.1)`% in 2025 Q4,
against `r scales::number(then, accuracy = 0.1)`% in
2022 Q1: a change of
`r scales::number(now - then, accuracy = 0.1, style_positive = "plus")`
points.

The data cover:

- 13 regions and the Kingdom as a whole
- 16 quarters, from 2022 Q1 to 2025 Q4

@sec-method defines the rate.

Official figures are on the [GASTAT website](https://www.stats.gov.sa).

## Method {#sec-method}

::: {.callout-note collapse="true" title="How the rate is defined"}
The unemployment rate is

$$
u = \frac{U}{U + E} \times 100
$$

where $U$ is the number unemployed and $E$ the number employed.
:::
