From the zip to your own report

  1. Unpack the zip and open the folder in Positron
  2. Render report.qmd; for another region, quarto render report.qmd -P region:Riyadh
  3. Render slides.qmd for the briefing
  4. Put your own data in data/ and your own sentences in the text
  5. theme_gastat() and tt_gastat() give every chart and table the house style

Instructor · the quiz roomThe quizzes, live in the deck, with a leaderboard

Every quiz in the deck plays live. → opens the question and the clock runs on every screen; the reveal shows how the room voted and who was fastest. After each quiz the leaderboard climbs on the same slide, and the speed games play themselves. One leaderboard runs through the three days. It runs on your laptop with Python and nothing else, and needs no internet.

Running it

  1. Download the whole course (the button below) and unzip it on the laptop you present from.
  2. In the course folder, run python3 room/room.py. It prints the addresses.
  3. Open http://localhost:8080/deck and present as usual. Each day starts on a join slide: participants scan the code on the same Wi-Fi as your laptop. If the network keeps devices apart, share your phone's hotspot.
  4. On a quiz slide, → opens the question and → again shows the answer before the time is up; then → moves on. After a quiz's last question, → shows the leaderboard.
  5. Award a passed done test (+500) or work shown to the room (+300) from http://localhost:8080/host.

A right answer earns 500 points, and up to 500 more for speed. Each right answer in a row after the first adds 50, up to 250.

The scores stay in room/room-state.json: close the room at the end of the day and start it again tomorrow to carry on. Names never leave your laptop. Opened anywhere else, the deck is the ordinary deck.
