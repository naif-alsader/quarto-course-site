1
Ctrl+Shift+P opens the command palette, where every command lives. Quarto: New Document starts a report.
2
A Quarto document begins with a header between two --- lines: here a title and the output format.
3
Under the header: your words, then R code in a chunk, fenced by three backticks. The same R you ran in the script.
4
Press Preview.
5
Quarto runs the code and shows the finished page in the Viewer: the words, and the chart drawn fresh. One file made it all.
6
Up close: the title from the header, the sentence you wrote, and the chart R drew. Edit the file, press Preview again, and the page follows.
