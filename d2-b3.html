02Try it

Your turn · 5 minutes

Brand your report

  1. Download _brand.yml and the two Tajawal fonts into a fonts folder next to your report.
  2. Render to HTML and to Typst: the colours and fonts change, report.qmd does not.
  3. Make a two-line report-ar.qmd with lang: ar and dir: rtl and render it.
_brand.ymlTajawal-Regular.ttfTajawal-Bold.ttf_language-ar.yml
color:
  palette:
    indigo: "#4137A8"
    green: "#00803A"
    dark: "#2C2D3F"
  primary: indigo
  foreground: dark

typography:
  fonts:
    - family: Tajawal
      source: file
      files:
        - path: fonts/Tajawal-Regular.ttf
          weight: 400
        - path: fonts/Tajawal-Bold.ttf
          weight: 700
  base: Tajawal
  headings:
    family: Tajawal
    weight: 700
    color: indigo

logo:
  medium: gastat-logo-color.png
